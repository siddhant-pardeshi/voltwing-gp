# How to play Voltwing GP

## Objective

Finish three laps of the dusk circuit faster than the five rival drones. Your best total time is saved on this device — the results screen tracks your personal best, and the position you finish (P1–P6) is based on total distance covered, so every gate and every clean corner counts.

## The cockpit

- **Velocity** — your speed in km/h. Top out around 322 km/h; boost pushes past it.
- **Lap / Pos** — current lap of 3, and your live race position.
- **Boost cell** — the meter in the top right. Boosting drains it; racing clean refills it slowly.
- **Lap time** — running clock for the current lap.

## Controls

### Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` | Bank left / right |
| `Shift` or `Space` | Boost (hold) |
| `Esc` / `P` | Pause |
| `M` | Mute |

### Touch

- **Left joystick** steers.
- **BOOST** button on the right fires the boost cell (hold).

## The boost cell — the whole game in one meter

1. **Racing clean recharges it.** Smooth lines, no contact, no off-track dust.
2. **Orbit gates supercharge it.** Every gate you fly through adds a big chunk of charge and a burst of speed. They appear on straights — set up your line a corner early.
3. **Boosting spends it fast.** The surge is strongest when your cell is full and the throttle is already open. Boost *out* of a corner, not into it.

A common rhythm: charge through two gates, spend it all on one straight overtake, rebuild during the next set of bends.

## Reading the track

- **The neon edges** alternate cyan and magenta. When the color band changes, a corner is starting — that is your cue to settle the car before you turn in.
- **Light pylons** line both sides. If you are seeing them up close, you are off the strip; the car drags through plasma dust and bleeds speed hard.
- **Curves lean the car outward — gently.** The bends are readable: edges glow in one continuous color, corners flow instead of snapping, and you can always steer back. Hold the stick into the bend and the car holds the line with you.
- **Hills hide gates.** On the crest you see less sky, not more track — lift slightly and hold your line until the far side reveals itself.

## Racing the drones

- The five rivals (Kestrel, Volt-7, Halo, Duskrun, Moth) each hold a pace and wobble around their line. They are faster on lap 3 than lap 1 — so are you, if you keep the cell charged.
- **Contact is a tax.** Bumping a drone cuts your speed to a fraction of theirs. A lost position costs less time than a collision — leave the pass for the next bend.
- **They can be beat in the corners.** Drones hold near-ideal lines but never boost. A full cell spent on the inside of a long bend gains more than any straight.
- Position updates live. If you are P4 with half a lap left, the two drones ahead are close — hunt gates, not noses.

## Failure and recovery

There is no death — only lost time:

- **Off-track:** heavy slowdown + dust. Steer gently back; stabbing the stick overcorrects.
- **Collision:** big speed loss + screen shake. Straighten, breathe, rebuild.
- **Empty cell at the wrong moment:** coast a straight, catch the next gate. It is never more than a few seconds away.

## Strategy quick-reference

1. Set up gates from the corner *before* them — enter the arch centered, not at its edge.
2. Boost down the *inside* of bends, not just straights: you gain positions where drones can't respond.
3. On lap 3 the drones speed up. Keep a half-cell in reserve for the final straight.
4. Watch your own lap time, not just position. Consistency beats heroics across three laps.
